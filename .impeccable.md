## Design Context

### Users
Established Zambian business owners, primarily in logistics & freight, hospitality, education, ICT & professional services, creative & production, retail/trade, and private healthcare — the sectors listed in `INDUSTRIES` in `lib/schemas.ts`, which is the source of truth; keep this line in sync with it. They're browsing during work hours on phones and laptops, likely frustrated by unreliable freelancers, and looking for a professional digital partner who can deliver predictable lead generation. They want systems, not one-off projects.

### Brand Personality
Confident, systematic, warm — trusted advisor energy. Professional authority with approachable warmth. Not cold or corporate, but not casual either.

### Positioning (affects copy, not just visuals)
The promise is **ownership**: we install a lead engine inside your business and hand it over. Not a retainer, not freelancer hours, built to keep running without us. Copy must not contradict this — avoid subscription/rental framing anywhere on the marketing site.

The Prospect Engine (our own prospect-research software) is currently positioned as *the reason the service is better*, not as a product for sale. Productising it is a later, deliberate step; until then the site should not imply it can be bought standalone, and there is no product or pricing page to send people to.

### Aesthetic Direction
- **Emotional goal**: Relief + confidence — "Finally, someone who gets it"
- **Theme**: Both light and dark (system preference). Both are fully defined in `app/globals.css`; design and check every surface in both.
- **Visual tone**: Professional, structured, warm — should feel like a well-run consultancy, not a flashy startup
- **Anti-patterns**: Generic agency templates, overly techy/cold aesthetics, freelancer vibes, fake browser chrome, perpetual/looping animation, procedurally generated imagery
- **Fonts**: Source Sans 3 (body), Epilogue (display), Geist Mono (code)

### Colour
`app/globals.css` is the source of truth — read it before designing; do not take hex values from this file alone.

- **Deep Purple `#2D1B69`** (`--primary`) is the brand colour: headline emphasis, primary buttons, `--chart-1`. In dark mode it becomes `#c088d8`.
- **Foreground is `#101a40`, not purple.** Body and headline text is navy; purple is the accent *within* it.
- **Burnt Orange `#b85a35`** (`--accent`) is a small-surface fill only — badges and focus states. It has never been used as text and must not be used for headlines. Do not describe it as a co-equal brand colour.
- **Molecular accents** (cyan `#6dd5ea`, purple `#b06cc8`, orange `#e07848`, yellow `#ecd04a`) are the data-visualisation palette, registered as `--chart-2` through `--chart-5`. Use them in charts and product UI, not as page decoration.
- **Known gap**: in dark mode `--chart-1` (`#c088d8`) and `--chart-3` (`#b06cc8`) are near-identical and cannot be told apart in a legend or a 3px rule. Avoid relying on that pair in dark.

### Design Principles
1. **Systems over flash** — Every visual element should reinforce the "we build reliable systems" message
2. **Warm authority** — Professional enough to trust with your business, warm enough to feel like a partner
3. **Clarity first** — Business owners are busy; every section should communicate value in seconds
4. **Zambian context** — Design for the local market; respect the audience's sophistication without being patronizing
5. **Progressive trust** — Build confidence through the page flow: problem → solution → proof → action
6. **Earn every claim** — No invented numbers, fake logos, placeholder testimonials or unverifiable timings. If a figure can't be sourced, cut the element rather than filling it.
7. **Motion runs once, and only on `transform` or `opacity`** — Entry animations play on arrival and stop. Nothing loops perpetually, with one deliberate exception: the services ticker under the hero, which the client chose and which pauses on hover. Everything respects `prefers-reduced-motion`. Anything that loops must animate a compositor property. The hero grid once drifted on `background-position`, which repaints the layer every frame: idle, with nothing scrolling and no JavaScript running, it held a throttled phone's main thread at 1927ms out of every 2000ms and scrolling ran at 12fps with every frame dropped. On `transform` the same effect costs 14ms and holds 60fps. If a looping animation needs a property that is not `transform` or `opacity`, the answer is a different animation.
8. **One loud action per screen** — The page's calls to action are `FlowButton` (`components/ui/flow-button.tsx`). `solid` is the single loudest thing in a viewport; `outline` is for persistent chrome, which is why the header CTA is outlined while the hero CTA below it is filled. Two solid purple pills in one screen compete and neither wins.
9. **Content must never depend on JavaScript** — Do not gate visible content behind a hydrated animation. Entrance motion animates *from an already-visible default*, so a stalled, slow or failed bundle still renders the page. This audience browses on mid-range phones over mobile data.
